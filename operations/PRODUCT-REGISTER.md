## Portfolio notes

### TheReplayMAG
The culture venture in the portfolio. It should remain clearly distinct from the software products while benefiting from shared Group operations where useful.

### Koroko
Long-term infrastructure bet. Current software maturity is meaningful, but product value depends heavily on underlying address and location data quality. NIPOST API/data access has been requested and should be tracked as an external dependency rather than assumed.

### Oroko
Primary near-term product focus through the end of 2026. It is also being used as an internal operating tool to document Group ventures and customer-discovery work, creating direct product validation from real usage.

### Ogoro
Energy operations intelligence. Keep the early validation software-first and use customer discovery to determine which operational workflows create the strongest value before expanding hardware or integration scope.

### Igho
Payroll infrastructure initially validated inside the Group, with a likely external wedge around small businesses, projects, creative productions, events and other teams that need reliable project-based payroll without a full HR platform.
