01 · Brand idea

Clarity before decoration

Structured

Hierarchy, alignment and relationships are immediately legible.

Quiet

Oroko does not compete with the user’s process. Loud colour, excessive cards and ornamental UI are excluded.

Operational

The product is designed around real work, ownership, procedures, inputs, outputs and governance.

02 · Identity

The mark is precise

Use

The mark stays crisp, isolated and aligned. Gradients, shadows and decorative process lines are not applied to the logo.

03 · Colour

Neutral surfaces, strong hierarchy

04 · Layout

Canvas first. Interface second.

Auto-layout

Oroko uses orderly process structures rather than freeform placement.

Inspector patterns

Details live in clean side panels and structured records, not scattered floating popovers.

Density with restraint

Detailed information remains readable through consistent spacing and hierarchy.

06 · Voice

Plain, useful and exact

Use process language

Steps, owners, decisions, procedures, inputs and outputs are named plainly.

No automation hype

Oroko documents and clarifies work. It is not positioned as an everything-workflow engine.

Make complexity understandable

Oroko explains without oversimplifying. The language removes ambiguity.
