01 · Brand idea

Clarity before decoration

Structured

Hierarchy, ownership and relationships should be immediately legible.

Human

Warm paper, restrained spacing and plain language stop serious software from feeling sterile.

Operational

The system is built around steps, owners, procedures, inputs, outputs and governance.

02 · Identity

The mark stays precise

Use

Keep the mark crisp and isolated. Do not add gradients, shadows or decorative process lines to the identity itself.

03 · Colour

Warm neutrals, one strong accent

Ink#1E1B18
White#FFFFFF
Paper#FAF8F4
Oroko Orange#FF6200
04 · Product language

Canvas first. Chrome second.

Canvas

Use the dot grid, structured nodes and clear selected states. The work surface should feel intentional, not decorative.

Inspector

Contextual detail belongs in disciplined side panels with predictable field hierarchy.

Navigation

Keep chrome quiet. Active state may use a pale surface plus a narrow orange line.

06 · Voice

Plain, useful and exact

Use process language

Name steps, owners, decisions, procedures, inputs and outputs plainly.

No automation hype

Oroko documents and clarifies work. It is not positioned as an everything-workflow engine.

Make complexity legible

Explain clearly without oversimplifying. The language should reduce ambiguity.
