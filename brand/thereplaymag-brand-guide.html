Brand guide · 2026

For the people.
By the people.

Independent culture publishing with an editorial voice that feels current, direct and close to the scenes it covers.

Culture first. Editorial judgement always.

01 · Brand idea

Underground without looking unfinished

Independent

Replay should feel self-directed, culturally aware and uninterested in generic media polish.

Editorial

Typography, pacing and imagery should always feel publication-led rather than platform-led.

Alive

Layouts can carry tension, scale shifts and bold interruptions, but should never become chaotic for its own sake.

02 · Logo

The wordmark leads

Use

Give the wordmark enough room to read immediately. Do not shrink it into a decorative corner when Replay itself is the subject.

03 · Colour

High contrast, selective energy

Ink#101010
White#FFFFFF

04 · Type

Headlines should feel printed, not templated

New music.
New voices.
No filler.
Display

Use bold condensed or heavy grotesk-style display typography with assertive scale and tight spacing.

Body

Keep editorial body copy highly readable and neutral. Let the headline and imagery provide personality.

05 · Image language

People, scenes and real culture

Put the subject
before the template.
Photography

Prefer authored, documentary, performance, portrait and scene-led imagery. Avoid generic stock photography and over-produced lifestyle imagery.

06 · Voice

Direct, informed, never try-hard

Write like a publication

Specific, confident, culturally literate. No corporate filler.

Do not imitate slang

Voice comes from editorial judgement, not forced informality.

Keep the subject central

The artist, work, scene or idea should remain more important than Replay itself.
