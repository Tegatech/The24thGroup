Brand guide · 2026

For the people.
By the people.

TheReplayMAG is an independent culture publication with a direct editorial voice and a visual language rooted in the people and scenes it covers.

Culture first. Editorial judgement always.

01 · Brand idea

Underground without looking unfinished

Independent

Replay is self-directed, culturally aware and distinct from generic media polish.

Editorial

Typography, pacing and imagery are publication-led. The interface never becomes the story.

Alive

Layouts use tension, scale shifts and bold interruption without becoming chaotic.

02 · Logo

The wordmark leads

Use

The wordmark is given enough room to read immediately. It is not reduced to a decorative corner when Replay is the primary brand.

03 · Colour

High contrast, selective energy

Ink#101010
White#FFFFFF
Replay Orange#FFB900
Replay Green#0E5B43
04 · Type

Headlines feel printed, not templated

New music.
New voices.
No filler.
Display

Display type is bold, condensed or heavy-grotesk in character, with assertive scale and tight spacing.

Body

Editorial body copy remains neutral and highly readable. Headlines and imagery carry the visual personality.

05 · Image language

People, scenes and real culture

Put the subject
before the template.
Photography

Replay uses authored, documentary, performance, portrait and scene-led imagery. Generic stock and over-produced lifestyle photography are excluded.

06 · Voice

Direct, informed, never try-hard

Write like a publication

The voice is specific, confident and culturally literate. Corporate filler is excluded.

Do not imitate slang

The voice comes from editorial judgement, not forced informality.

Keep the subject central

The artist, work, scene or idea remains more important than Replay itself.
