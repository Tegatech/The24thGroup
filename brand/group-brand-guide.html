01 · Brand idea

What the Group stands for

The brand follows the operating thesis: make fragmented, manual or unreliable work clearer, more structured and more dependable.

Principle 01

Clarity

Strong hierarchy, direct language and deliberate spacing make the important thing easy to see.

Principle 02

Discipline

Consistency takes priority over decorative novelty. Every system feels intentional and maintained.

Principle 03

Long-term

The identity is built to mature. It does not depend on short-lived visual trends.

05 · Layout

Structured, editorial, spacious.

The Group uses strong grids, open space, fine rules and restrained surfaces rather than card-heavy SaaS layouts.

01

Alignment first

Pages use a consistent content width and clear vertical rhythm. Elements feel placed, never floated.

02

Fewer containers

Cards are functional tools, not decoration. Sections, rules and hierarchy are preferred when a container adds no meaning.

03

Warm surfaces sparingly

Paper and soft-accent surfaces create contrast and emphasis. They are not blanket backgrounds.

06 · Graphic language

Quiet geometry, not decoration.

Circles, fine rules, dots and technical/editorial structures are used only when they support hierarchy or meaning.

Orbit

Circles and nodes

The landing-page orbit is the reference for restrained geometric expression. It remains sparse and secondary.

Rules

Thin separators

Subtle grey rules create rhythm and structure instead of heavy boxes.

Contrast

Black statements

Dark sections are reserved for major statements, emphasis and strong transitions.

07 · Voice

Clear, measured, confident.

The Group speaks like an operator. Inflated startup language, generic consulting phrases and investor-first positioning are excluded.

Use

Plain and specific

“We build where important work is still fragmented, manual or unreliable.”

Avoid

Hype and abstraction

Do not use phrases such as “revolutionising industries” or “disrupting ecosystems” without grounded evidence.

08 · Digital & documents

One visual discipline across formats.

Web, Slate reports, documents and presentations share the same hierarchy, restraint and identity rules.

Web

Landing-page standard

Inter navigation, the canonical Group mark, IBM Plex Sans Condensed headlines and white/paper/black composition define the digital system.

Reports

Editorial intelligence

Reports use strong titles, metadata, evidence, concise status language, fine rules and clean tables.

Presentations

Large ideas, low noise

Presentations prioritise one idea per screen and avoid dense consultancy-deck formatting.

09 · Portfolio ownership

Powered by The 24th Group

Product identities remain primary. The ownership line connects them to the Group without making every product visually identical.

Product brand first. Group ownership second.

The ownership line appears on venture websites, product documentation and selected public surfaces where Group provenance matters. It is not used as the Group’s own tagline.

10 · Guardrails

Keep the system recognisable.

Consistency comes from discipline, not from forcing every venture into the same visual personality.

Do

  • Use the landing-page identity as the Group standard.
  • Use the canonical mark and Inter wordmark consistently.
  • Use IBM Plex Sans Condensed for display and Inter for body/UI.
  • Keep gold restrained.
  • Let each venture retain its own character.

Don’t

  • Introduce alternative Group lockups.
  • Use a different font for the Group wordmark.
  • Overuse cards, gradients or generic startup visuals.
  • Make product brands look like reskins of the Group site.
  • Use “Powered by The 24th Group” on Group-owned documents as a tagline.
