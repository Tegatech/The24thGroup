01 · Brand idea

Quiet confidence around money

Trustworthy

Payment, approval and employment records are dependable and easy to verify.

Procedural

The payroll run is understandable from preparation through reconciliation.

Human

The product handles money, but the outcome is people being paid correctly and having useful proof of employment.

02 · Identity

05 · Documents

Useful outside Igho

Payslips

Payslips are clean, formal and easy to understand, prioritising employer, employee, period, gross pay, net pay and payment details.

Employment proof

Employment documents are credible enough for practical use in applications and verification.

Records

Dates, statuses and transaction outcomes remain legible in exports and print.

06 · Voice

Clear about money, calm about problems

Use exact amounts

Important payment information is never hidden behind vague labels.

Explain exceptions

Igho states what failed, who is affected and what needs to happen next.

No finance jargon

Payroll language is written for ordinary employers and workers to understand.
