01 · Brand idea

Quiet confidence around money

Trustworthy

Payment, approval and employment records should be dependable and easy to verify.

Procedural

The payroll run should be understandable from preparation through reconciliation.

Human

The system handles money, but the outcome is people being paid correctly and having usable records.

04 · Product expression

Dense information, disciplined hierarchy

Surfaces

Use dark navigation, paper app backgrounds and white data surfaces with small radii and restrained shadow.

Records

Prefer tables, strips, rows and side panels when comparison and operational detail matter.

Colour roles

Navy carries primary actions, gold marks financial significance, and semantic colours are reserved for state.

06 · Voice

Clear about money, calm about problems

Use exact amounts

Important payment information should never be hidden behind vague labels.

Explain exceptions

State what failed, who is affected and what needs to happen next.

No finance jargon

Write payroll language for ordinary employers and workers to understand.
