01 · Brand idea

Quiet confidence around money

Trustworthy

Payment, approval and employment records should feel dependable and easy to verify.

Procedural

Igho should make the payroll run understandable from preparation through reconciliation.

Human

The product handles money, but the outcome is people being paid correctly and having useful proof of employment.

02 · Identity

The dot grid is the signature

igho
Centre dot

The gold centre dot is the distinguishing accent. Keep the other dots in deep ink and preserve the simple 3 × 3 geometry.

03 · Colour

UI principle

Use clear stages, totals, exceptions and evidence. Avoid making payroll look like a generic banking dashboard.

05 · Documents

Simple enough to use outside Igho

Payslips

Clean, formal and easy to understand. Prioritise employer, employee, period, gross/net pay and payment details.

Employment proof

Documents should be credible enough for practical use such as applications and verification.

Records

Dates, statuses and transaction outcomes should remain legible in exports and print.

06 · Voice

Clear about money, calm about problems

Use exact amounts

Never hide important payment information behind vague labels.

Explain exceptions

Tell the operator what failed, who is affected and what needs to happen next.

Avoid finance jargon

Use payroll language ordinary employers and workers can understand.
