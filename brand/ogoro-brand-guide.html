01 · Brand idea

Operational awareness that leads to action

Alert

Ogoro should surface what matters now, without turning every reading into an alarm.

Practical

Design around equipment, fuel, runtime, incidents, cost and action, not abstract analytics.

Reliable

The system should feel calm enough to trust in daily operations and strong enough for critical environments.

02 · Identity

The frog is the signal

Character

The Ogoro mark can carry more personality than the interface. Keep product UI disciplined so the frog remains distinctive rather than becoming decorative noise.

03 · Colour

05 · Type & data

Readable under pressure

DM Sans

Primary brand and interface family. Friendly enough for everyday use, disciplined enough for operational software.

Numbers matter

Runtime, fuel, cost, load and incident data need strong numeric hierarchy and consistent units.

Status is semantic

Colour should reinforce a condition but never be the only way a state is communicated.

06 · Voice

Calm, useful, action-oriented

Tell users what changed

Prefer “Fuel dropped below 40%” to vague “Performance issue” language.

Recommend when useful

Insights should help the operator decide what to do next.

Avoid futuristic hype

Ogoro is operational technology for real environments. Credibility matters more than sounding advanced.
