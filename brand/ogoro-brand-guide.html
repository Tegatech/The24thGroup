01 · Brand idea

Operational awareness that leads to action

Alert

Surface what matters now without turning every reading into an alarm.

Practical

Focus on equipment, fuel, runtime, incidents, cost and next action, not abstract analytics.

Reliable

Calm enough for daily operations and strong enough for critical environments.

05 · Type & data

Readable under pressure

DM Sans

DM Sans is the primary family across brand and interface work.

Numbers matter

Runtime, fuel, cost, load and incident data need strong numeric hierarchy and consistent units.

Status is semantic

Colour reinforces state but never becomes the only way condition is communicated.

06 · Voice

Calm, useful and action-oriented

State what changed

Use specific language such as “Fuel dropped below 40%”, not vague issue labels.

Recommend when useful

Insights should help the operator decide what happens next.

No futuristic hype

Credibility in real operating environments matters more than sounding advanced.
