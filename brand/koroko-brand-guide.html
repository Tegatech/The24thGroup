01 / Brand idea

Trust through structure

Precise

Koroko uses clear labels, consistent fields and strong information hierarchy. Ambiguity is removed wherever possible.

Neutral

Evidence and utility create confidence. The data does the convincing.

Nigerian by purpose

Koroko is locally relevant by function and context, not by decorative national clichés.

02 / Identity

Compact, technical, recognisable

Primary behaviour

The Koroko icon is paired with a clean uppercase wordmark where the name must be explicit. Decorative map-pin effects are not added to the mark.

06 / Voice

Practical, direct, credible

Say what it does

Language is concrete and specific about addresses, verification, location and data quality.

No inflated claims

Vision can be ambitious. Product claims remain precise and supportable.

Explain confidence

Judgements are accompanied by a basis, status or score wherever the product supports it.
