01 / Brand idea

Trust through structure

Precise

Use clear labels, consistent fields and strong information hierarchy. Koroko should never feel vague.

Neutral

Confidence comes from evidence and utility, not hype. Let the data do the convincing.

Nigerian by purpose

The product should feel locally relevant without relying on clichés or decorative national symbolism.

02 / Identity

Compact, technical, recognisable

Primary behaviour

Use the Koroko icon with a clean uppercase wordmark treatment where the name needs to be explicit. Do not surround the mark with decorative map-pin effects.

Visual principle

Prefer tables, stat strips, field groups, maps and simple evidence panels over decorative dashboards.

06 / Voice

Practical, direct, credible

Say what it does

Use concrete language about addresses, verification, location and data quality.

Avoid inflated claims

Trust is weakened by vague superlatives. Be ambitious in vision and precise in product claims.

Explain confidence

Where Koroko makes a judgement, show the basis, status or score wherever possible.
