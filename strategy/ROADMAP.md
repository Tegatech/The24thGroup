# The 24th Group — Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally high level. Product-level implementation work belongs in the relevant product repo and Asana project.

## Now — August to December 2026

### Group
- Establish a repeatable monthly review process using GitHub, Asana and Group notes.
- Standardise the minimum operating files expected in product repositories.
- Keep the Group website current as a simple public index of what the Group builds.
- Begin documenting the future shared systems model across GitHub, Asana, Catalyst and Zoho.

### Oroko
- Primary product focus through the end of 2026.
- Use Oroko in day-to-day work as a source of real product validation.
- Use Oroko to document Group ventures and Ogoro customer-discovery work.
- Close core workflow issues around current/future state, decision branches, public share and file attachment guidance.
- Move from internal use toward a credible external commercialisation plan.

### Koroko
- Continue hardening the public product and API path.
- Complete remaining authentication, API gateway, workspace isolation, payment and operational-readiness evidence.
- Track the NIPOST API/data request and assess any response for coverage, fields, coordinates, update frequency, commercial rights and redistribution constraints.
- Keep investment focused on address/location-data quality and trusted infrastructure rather than feature volume.

### Ogoro
- Run structured customer discovery before deeper product expansion.
- Use Oroko to document interviews, workflows, pain points and emerging operating patterns.
- Validate the software-first wedge around energy operations, generator/fuel/maintenance visibility and useful alerts/reporting.
- Avoid premature hardware complexity.

### Igho
- Establish the dedicated repository and Asana project when implementation work resumes.
- Validate the complete payroll lifecycle internally.
- Focus on employee setup, funding, approval, transfers, transfer outcomes, payslips and records.
- Develop the positioning around small teams, projects, creative productions and events without expanding into a broad HR suite.

### TheReplayMAG
- Continue operating the publication.
- Document repeatable editorial and operating processes where doing so reduces dependency on informal knowledge.

## January 2027 onward

- Move to a clearer annual operating plan for the Group.
- Introduce Group-level funding when financially practical; the working intention discussed is a recurring Group commitment, with allocation rules to be defined separately.
- Consolidate appropriate shared marketing and operations expenditure at Group level.
- Use 2026 monthly reviews as the baseline for setting 2027 product priorities.
- Begin formalising the Zoho operating backbone so reporting and administrative data can feed a simpler Group-level view.

## Roadmap rules

1. Do not copy detailed engineering backlogs into this file.
2. Record outcomes and strategic gates, not every task.
3. A product can move slower than another product without being unhealthy.
4. Dependencies that require founder input should be explicit.
5. Update this roadmap when strategy changes, not simply because tasks were completed.
