# The 24th Group — Group Thesis

## Purpose

The 24th Group is the strategic home for a focused portfolio of ventures across culture, infrastructure and operational technology.

The Group exists to build and operate useful businesses with long-term intent, shared operating discipline and clear ownership.

## What connects the portfolio

The ventures are different, but they share a common pattern: they take fragmented, manual or unreliable systems and make them more structured, visible and dependable.

- **TheReplayMAG** — culture, creative communities and independent publishing.
- **Koroko** — location infrastructure and trusted address intelligence for Nigeria.
- **Oroko** — structured process mapping, documentation and operational clarity.
- **Ogoro** — operational intelligence for energy systems, facilities and physical assets.
- **Igho** — payroll infrastructure for projects, creative productions and small teams.

## Group model

The Group should remain lean. Shared capability should exist where it reduces duplication or founder load, especially across:

- reporting
- finance and administration
- brand governance
- marketing operations
- systems and tooling
- product operating standards

Each venture should remain close to its own customer, problem and market.

## Strategic principles

### Clarity
We focus on real problems and make the complex easier to understand.

### Discipline
We build deliberately, stay close to evidence and avoid unnecessary complexity.

### Long-term
We optimise for ownership, durability and useful systems that can compound over time.

## Capital approach

The Group is being structured to operate with discipline regardless of whether external capital is raised. The objective is not to build reporting for investors; it is to build a reliable operating history for the Group itself.

Future Group-level funding can be allocated across shared needs such as marketing, operations and product support without forcing each venture to duplicate those functions.

## Operating intent

The Group should become increasingly easy to understand from its records.

At any point, it should be possible to answer:

1. What are we building?
2. Why does each venture exist?
3. What changed this month?
4. What did we learn?
5. What is blocked?
6. What are we doing next?

This repository is the canonical home for that Group-level view.
